How to Use This Calculator
  1. Enter replacement cost: Use the estimated cost to rebuild the home, not its market value.
  2. Enter the amount of insurance carried: This is the dwelling coverage amount available for the classroom calculation.
  3. Enter the required insurance percentage: The project uses an 80% requirement.
  4. Enter the loss and deductible: The tool shows the project’s reimbursement and homeowner out-of-pocket amount step by step.
Understanding the Financial Concepts
Replacement cost is not market value

Replacement cost focuses on the cost to rebuild or replace property with similar materials, while market value also reflects land and market conditions.

Underinsurance matters

Carrying substantially less insurance than the amount required by the policy can reduce the reimbursement available for a covered partial loss.

Calculation Method & Assumptions

The PFI 3301 project uses a simplified coinsurance relationship. It first calculates the fraction of replacement cost that is insured, then compares the coverage carried with the required percentage and applies that relationship to the covered loss after the deductible.

Project reimbursement = [insurance carried ÷ (required % × replacement cost)] × (loss − deductible)

Policy language varies. Actual claim settlement depends on the contract, valuation method, limits, exclusions, endorsements, deductibles, and applicable law. This calculator is for the project model, not for estimating an actual insurance claim.

Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Using market value instead of replacement cost.
  • Forgetting to apply the deductible.
  • Treating “80%” as meaning the insurer always pays 80% of every claim.
  • Assuming the classroom formula overrides the terms of an actual homeowners policy.
